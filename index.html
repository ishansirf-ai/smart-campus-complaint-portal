ABOUT BGMIT MUDHOL

Learning with purpose. Growing with technology.

Welcome to Biluru Gurubasava Mahaswamiji Institute of Technology (BGMIT), Mudhol.

Established in 2013 under the prestigious lineage of B.V.V. Sangha, Bagalkot, BGMIT is dedicated to nurturing the next generation of engineers and management professionals.

Approved by AICTE, New Delhi, and affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi, the institution combines academic knowledge with practical skills and technological development.

OUR MISSION

To deliver quality technical education that empowers students from diverse backgrounds, fostering innovation, ethical values and technological excellence to serve society.

WHY CHOOSE BGMIT?

Education beyond the classroom

VTU Curriculum

Academic programs aligned with university standards and technical education requirements.

Modern Campus

A 15-acre campus on Mantur Road with laboratories, library facilities and student accommodation.

Industry Ready

A dedicated placement cell supporting soft-skills training, technical workshops and campus recruitment opportunities.

Holistic Growth

Encouraging extracurricular activities, sports, technical clubs and other campus activities.
